# Website Redesign Proposal Mahendras Online Website

## Introduction
Mahendra Educational Private Limited is a very famous name for government exam preparation in India. The institution has over 150 coaching centres across tier 1, tier 2, and tier 3 towns in India, and lakhs of students deeply trust the brand legacy. While the offline business is highly successful, the official website currently looks very old. It operates more like a crowded school notice board rather than a modern online learning platform.

Right now, new companies like Testbook and Adda247 are winning online market share because their websites are very clean and easy to use. The Mahendras website gets a lot of free traffic from Google searches because it regularly uploads daily current affairs and exam news. However, this traffic is being wasted because visitors leave the platform immediately without buying any courses or test series. This proposal explains the main problems found during my audit and gives a simple plan to fix them so online course sales can grow.

***

## Part 1 Website Audit What is Broken

### 1 Crowded Layout and Confusing Homepage
* Too Much Information The homepage has too many things happening at the same time. There are flashing text links, moving banners, and multiple unorganized sidebars. A student gets confused about where to click.
* Hard to Buy Courses If a student wants to buy a new Online Live Batch or check the price of a test series, they have to scroll past huge blocks of text. Important buttons like Buy Now or Watch Free Demo are hidden at the bottom of the page.

### 2 Confusing Menu Links
* Too Many Choices The top desktop primary navigation menu bar is completely packed. It lists 17 different standalone links with identical structural hierarchy, including Home, About Us, Blogs, News, Learning, Exam Notifications, Upcoming Exams, Events, Gallery, Careers, Offline Centres, Online Batches, and Student Login.
* The Result Because there are too many options, students face instant choice paralysis and early user drop offs, causing them to close the website.

### 3 Poor Mobile Experience and Slow Loading
* Bad for Mobile Users More than 80 percent of regional students from small towns study exclusively using their mobile phones. The current web architecture forces dense grids onto mobile screens, making links too small and tightly packed to tap accurately.
* Slow Speed The website takes too much time to load on normal cellular networks because the images are not compressed and there is too much heavy code behind the pages.

### 4 Wasting Free Traffic
* Dead Ends on Pages Daily informational resource pages like the Daily Current Affairs blogs secure exceptional organic traffic via Google search. However, once a student finishes reading the material, the page simply ends. There are no dynamic sidebar banners or floating panels matching course products to the specific topics being read, meaning the site gives out free information but captures no commercial sales.

***

## Part 2 The UIUX Redesign Plan How to Fix It

The messy notice board style must be removed to divide the web platform into 3 simple sections or hubs. A large, central program exploration search query box will sit at the top of the clean homepage, asking students exactly which exam they want to search for.

### The Streamlined 3 Hub Model
* The Learning Hub Consolidates all live video lecture schedules, upcoming batch dates, and paid course catalogs. It features an instant, form free demo class preview layout to let students try materials before registration.
* The Practice Hub Hosts the entire digital testing suite including premium mock exams, daily concept quizzes, and timed test packages. It connects directly to an advanced dashboard monitoring performance scores visually.
* The Update Hub Serves as the absolute center for official government recruitment news timelines, syllabus summaries, and eligibility calculators to act as the primary lead generation engine.

### Core Interface Architecture Updates
* Top Header Layout Replaced the historical crowded moving headline rows with a minimalist presentation focused entirely on a clean search bar asking Which exam are you preparing for today.
* Course Pages Replaced plain paragraph listings that required multi field login steps with modern course cards tracking price figures, durations, teachers, and a quick Watch 5 Min Demo button.
* Student Reviews Shifted old text quotes hidden deep inside pages into a neat row of short video testimonials of successful students sharing their stories.
* Student Marks Card Upgraded simple text showing right and wrong answers into smart report cards with colorful pie charts and trend graphs tracking speed and weak topics.

## Part 3 Simple Marketing Tricks to Increase Sales

To turn regular website visitors into paying customers, the platform needs to guide students step by step through an active marketing funnel.

* Pop Up Quizzes on Free Pages When a student reads the Daily Current Affairs page, a small box will appear on the side asking them to test their memory with a five minute quiz. To see their final score, the student enters a mobile number and email, giving the sales team a fresh contact lead.
* Ad Tracking Remarketing Pixels If a student spends time reading an exam analysis page but leaves without buying anything, tracking codes will place targeted ads on their Facebook, YouTube, or Instagram feeds showing top teachers solving relevant exam questions.
* Creating Urgency Live countdown clocks will be added to course checkout pages stating that discounts or seats are expiring soon, encouraging students to complete their purchase quickly.

## Part 4 Technology and Safe Launch Plan

### 1 Technology Choices
* Modern Coding Framework Build the front end of the website using React.js or Next.js so that pages load instantly even on weak mobile internet connections in village areas.
* Strong Database Use PostgreSQL for the backend database so the website does not crash when lakhs of students check their exam results at the same time.
* Fast Content Delivery Network Use Cloudflare to save copies of text and images on nearby servers across India so pages open fast anywhere.

### 2 The 10 Week Implementation Timeline
* Weeks 1 and 2 Planning Draw simple sketches of the new pages on paper and show them to a few students at local physical branches to verify layout usability.
* Weeks 3 to 5 Building Frontend modular programming to write the code for the new pages, design the video player, and build the central student analytics dashboard.
* Weeks 6 to 8 Testing Launch a hidden closed beta test website deployed exclusively for current classroom students to find and fix any broken buttons or bugs.
* Weeks 9 and 10 Final Launch Perform a cross device responsiveness audit, execute permanent 301 route mapping, and launch the new system for the public.

### 3 Keeping Google Rankings Safe SEO Protection
Mahendras ranks on the first page of Google for highly competitive terms. To preserve historic search authority and protect free organic traffic during the redesign, three controls will be maintained.
* Unchanged URL Slugs Existing popular blog pages will maintain their historical address definitions perfectly so search algorithms verify brand expertise.
* System Redirect Auditing Moved submenus will employ direct permanent 301 redirect links so that anyone clicking an old address shifts to the new page cleanly.
* Technical Content Maintenance Full textual resource lists and detailed educational text remain readable so that Google continues to rank the site high.
